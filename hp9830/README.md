# Hewlett-Packard 9830

The HP 9830 was a programmable desktop calculator and BASIC-oriented
computer in Hewlett-Packard's 9810/20/30 series. It extended the calculator
family toward general-purpose desk computing while retaining
microprogrammed arithmetic, storage, and peripheral interfaces.

Bluespec implementation: bsv/ (not started).

See [`plan.md`](plan.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md). The initial
source is Chapter 49, printed p. 801, of *Computer Structures: Principles and
Examples* (1982).
