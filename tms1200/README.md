# Texas Instruments TMS 1200

The TMS 1200 was a related monolithic microcomputer in Texas Instruments'
calculator-oriented family. It combined processor, memory, control, and I/O
resources on a chip, making the instruction-set and microprogram organization
part of a tightly constrained embedded system.

Bluespec implementation: bsv/ (not started).

See [`plan.md`](plan.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md). The initial
source is Chapter 34, printed p. 587, of *Computer Structures: Principles and
Examples* (1982).
