# Golden 020 — a folded unconditional JMP costs nothing

Table 4-32 (printed p. 4-3) gives `JMP` "Min 0, Max 1": 0 when the jump
is folded into the instruction before it (p. 4-7), 1 when it is not.
This trace is one folded `JMP`: a three-parcel `MOV`, the one-parcel
`JMP` that folds into it, an instruction that is jumped over, and the
target.

Until 2026-11-20 the Bluespec core charged a folded `JMP` 3 and really
squashed behind it: its table of predicted directions listed the two
predicted-taken *conditional* jumps and not `JMP`, so a folded `JMP` was
prefetched down its fall-through and then "mispredicted" every time.
Nothing was wrong with the values, and no earlier golden folds an
unconditional jump, so nothing failed; the record-by-record comparison
of 1st Edition Plan 9's `za` showed it as 3,808 records agreeing in
everything but `cyc`.

The image is 4 words = 16 bytes; 3 records retire.

## How this file was derived

By hand from the printed tables, before either model ran it.  Neither
`asm/` nor `sem/` nor `bsv/` produced or corrected any field.

## Pre-state

The fixed pre-state of `README.md`: PC `0x00000000`, PSW `0x00000200`,
SP `0x00000100`, MSP `0x00000180`, memory zero except the image.

## Retirement lines

A folded branch is its own record (p. 4-3 note; `sem/README.md`), after
its host's.

L01. `HRI pc=0000 insn=86fd0001 sp=00000100>00000100 msp=00000180>00000180 we=1 addr=00000104 data=00000001 mask=0f trap=0 cyc=1`

Cycles: 1 — `MOV` min=max=1 (Table 4-32, p. 4-3).

Transition (p. 3-32): `SP+4 = 0x104` receives 1.

L02. `HRI pc=0006 insn=0c040000 sp=00000100>00000100 msp=00000180>00000180 we=0 addr=00000000 data=00000000 mask=00 trap=0 cyc=0`

Cycles: 0 — `JMP` folded = 0 (Table 4-32, p. 4-3: Min 0, Max 1; the note gives 0 folded, 1 unfolded).

Transition (p. 3-27; p. 4-7): one-parcel `JMP 0x000e`, offset `+4` parcels from its own address.  The instruction before it is a three-parcel dyadic `MOV`, which p. 4-7 lets a one-parcel branch fold into, and an unconditional jump is not a prediction that can be wrong: no squash, no penalty.  The `MOV $0xdead, R8` at `0x0008` is jumped over and does not retire.

L03. `HRI pc=000e insn=2c020000 sp=00000100>00000100 msp=00000180>00000180 we=0 addr=00000000 data=00000000 mask=00 trap=0 cyc=1`

Cycles: 1 — `NOP` min=max=1 (Table 4-36, p. 4-5).

Transition (p. 3-35): no write.

## Discriminating check (observed 2026-11-20)

With `OP_JMP` removed from `predictedTaken` again (`HobbitTypes.bsv`,
the state before), `bsv/run_golden.sh 020` printed L02 with `cyc=3` and
nothing else differed; restored, all three lines match.  On 1st Edition
Plan 9's `za main9.s` the same change is 3,808 records' `cyc`
(121,302 of 125,572 agreeing before, 125,110 after).
