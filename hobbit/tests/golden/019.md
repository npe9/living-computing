# Golden 019 — two pointed-to operands, indirect CALL and JMP, ENTER's memory targets, DQM with a constant

Five things 1st Edition Plan 9's `echo` and `za` execute within their
first few hundred instructions and that the Bluespec core refused or did
not have until 2026-11-20:

* **both operands indirect** (`CMPEQ *R4:UB, *R8:UB`, and a `MOV` between
  two pointed-to bytes): Table 1-21's modes `0x8`..`0xB` on both sides
  (p. 1-25).  The decoder used to refuse every such form but `MOVA` and a
  word-destination `MOV`;
* **`CALL *Roffset`** (Table 1-23 mode `0xD`, "stack offset indirect",
  p. 1-26) and **`JMP **$addr`** (mode `0xC`, "absolute indirect"): the
  target is a word of the stack or of memory;
* **`ENTER *$addr` and `ENTER *Roffset`** (p. 3-20, cases 4 and 3): "the
  target value ... is fetched from memory (or the stack cache)" and the
  cache is flushed unconditionally and left empty (p. 3-19);
* **`DQM $const, dst`** (p. 3-18): the constant replicated two or four
  times, the size taken from the destination mode field.

The image is 46 words = 184 bytes; 33 records retire.

## How this file was derived

In three steps, each by hand from the printed pages.  L01..L18 and a
final `NOP` were derived before either model ran the image.  L19..L21
(the later words of the two DQM blocks) were added after both models had
reproduced those lines, because nothing read the words a DQM writes after
its first.  L22..L33 were added after the first injection campaign showed
four bugs the image still let through: a wrong word for the second
pointed-to operand (the flag was never used and the byte store never read
back), a quad word with its third word missing, a DQM whose later words
land after the next instruction has read them, and three operand latches
where four are needed.  The added lines were derived the same way as the
first: the parcels
from Tables 1-15, 1-18, 1-19, 1-20, 1-21 and 1-23, the values and
pointers from the operation descriptions cited on each line.  The cycle
rows are Chapter 4's; `Q` and `D` are the README's named memory
parameters (model choices, 4 and 2).  Neither `asm/` nor `sem/` nor
`bsv/` produced or corrected any field.

p. 1-24); five-parcel: `11 | ...` with 32-bit fields (Table 1-20,
p. 1-25); three-parcel monadic: opcode `0x00`, the mode in the smode
nibble and the subcode in the low nibble, one 32-bit operand (Table 1-19,
p. 1-24; p. 1-23).  Opcodes: `MOV` `0x06`, `DQM` `0x07`, `CMPEQ` `0x1f`;
