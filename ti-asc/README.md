# Texas Instruments ASC

The Texas Instruments ASC was a highly modular supercomputer architecture.
Its design used multiple specialized processing modules and a scalable
interconnection structure to increase throughput, illustrating a different
tradeoff from the tightly integrated scalar and vector supercomputers of its
period.

Bluespec implementation: bsv/ (not started).

See [`plan.md`](plan.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md). The initial
source is Chapter 45, printed p. 753, of *Computer Structures: Principles and
Examples* (1982).
