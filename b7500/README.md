# Burroughs B7500

The Burroughs B7500 is the larger member of the B6500 stack-oriented family.
Its tagged data, Polish-string operator syllables, top-of-stack registers, and
protected procedure stack support language-based multiprogramming and
recursive execution.

Bluespec implementation: bsv/ (not started).

See [`plan.md`](plan.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md). The initial
source is Chapter 16, printed p. 244, of *Computer Structures: Principles and
Examples* (1982); it remains distinct from the B6500 and B5000 entries.
