1 Abstract

Research Title :
	``Distribution Project of IFS Japanese Morphological Grammar Rules''

Project Reader :
	SANO Hiroshi,
		Lecturer, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies

Contents :
     In 1996, under the assistance of the AITEC's research
funding program, we have developed the IFS (ICOT Free
Software) Japanese Morphological Grammar Rules and its
user/operation manuals.

     For its wide use, the tool includes:

(1) GUI-based software for Microsoft Windows(R).
    This includes the environment for testing the morphological
    grammar rules, by which users can easily modify grammar
    rules to suit their needs.
(2) The manuals, providing an efficient and powerful help for
    the users.

2 The function of the software

i. Application Area
	Natural language processing
ii. Function
	GUI-based front-end software used for morphological analysis of Japanese
	IFS Japanese Morphological Grammar Rules
iii. Software image
	(None)
