List of ICOT Free Software

Institute for New Generation Computer Technology (ICOT) released 100 software programs during 1992 to 1994 as ICOT Free Software.

Since almost IFS requires machines that had been developed in FGCS project, it is hard to execute them on current machines.

In this page, we will release all of IFS mainly for displaying what kind of sofware programs had been researched and developed in FGCS project and its follow-on project. Ideas behind them can also be found.

For softwares that can still be researched, developed, and maintained, please refer to this page.
