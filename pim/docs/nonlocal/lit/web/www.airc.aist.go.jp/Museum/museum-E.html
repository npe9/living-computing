FGCS Digital Archive


Welcome to FGCS Digital Archive!


This archive shows research results and historical papers of the following projects and activities for 17 years:

  1. FGCS Project carried out by Institute for New Generation Computer Technology (ICOT) from FY1982 to FY1992 and the Two Year Follow-up Project from FY1993 to FY1994.

  2. Activities carried out by Department of FGCS Technology of Research Institute for Advanced Information Technology (AITEC) as a subsidiary organization to Japan Information Processing Development Center (JIPDEC) from FY1995 to FY1998.


Software released here contain some software programs that cannot be executed on current machine environment since machine environment in which the software were developped was changed. However, they are effective as examples for implementation of advance researchs of Parallel/Knowledge Processing Technology, for example using Parallel machines, Reasoning or Learning. Let's access these software.
