Introduction

Go to INDEX

The KLIC Programming Contest is back again this year.
Test your KL1 programming ability with KLIC!

We have expand the contest this year to enable more people to enjoy KL1 programming, but this will be the final contest so we look forward to your entry!

1. Contents of Contest

Go to INDEX

There are the following three entry categories in the contest. Select a category of interest (you can enter more than one category).

(1) ENTRY CATEGORY: KL1 users from novice to expert can try KL1 programming to solve a given subject. Those who get the correct answer will be candidates for an award.

(2) SPEED CATEGORY: Demonstrate your programming performance in either a sequential environment or parallel environment.

(3) IDEA CATEGORY: We set no subject in this category . You create a program to solve your own subject in either a sequential environment or parallel environment. We will primarily judge the uniqueness of your idea.

- Copyright The copyrights for submitted programs shall belong to the applicants. However, please note that AITEC reserves the right to use the winning programs without payment, for advertisement purposes.

2. Award Announcement

Go to INDEX

(1) ENTRY CATEGORY: A programming subject and a checking program will be announced. Programs which solve the given subject correctly with a checking program to be published will be candidates for receiving an award.

-- Supplementary prize: 20,000 yen for a program, for up to 50 programs If more than 50 programs are award candidates, we will choose 50 from among them by lottery, and those that are not chosen by the lottery will receive a book token worth 5,000 yen for domestic applicants, for up to 100 programs (a check for the equivalent amount for overseas applicants).
*One person or one group can receive a book token for only one program.

In order to offer participants a better chance of winning an award in the Entry Category, we will choose nominees from among those who have not received any awards in the past or this contest, in case awards are chosen by lottery.

(2) SPEED CATEGORY and IDEA CATEGORY: The submitted programs will be reviewed by the judging committee of this contest according to its selection standards, and first and second prizes and honorable mentions will be chosen in each category. The award for each prize is as follows:

-- Supplementary prizes for Speed Category and Idea Category:
  • 1st prize (supplementary prize: 300,000 yen for 1 program)
  • 2nd prize (supplementary prize: 100,000 yen for 3 programs)
  • Honorable mention (supplementary prize: 50,000 yen for 6 programs)
Participation prize: a book token worth 5,000 yen for domestic applicants (a check for the equivalent amount for overseas applicants)
The judging committee will select this award program according to its selection standards.

The award information will be informed to all participants by e-mail by December 5. The "Cyber" Awards Ceremony will be held on the WWW.

3. How to Participate and Schedule

 Go to INDEX

(1) How to Participate in the Contest

Please send us your program that solves the subjects published late in July, according to the submission guides that will be announced together with the subjects. The programs should reach the contest secretariat by October 31.

The contest is open to anyone who wishes to participate. Novice KL1 users are especially welcome. The KLIC tutorial text is on the KLIC WWW page for your reference.

(2) Schedule (Provisional)
  • Announcement of Subjects and Submission Guides: Late in July, 1998 (to be decided)
  • Deadline for Program Submission: October 31, 1998
  • Awards Announcement Date: November 30 (to be decided)
  • Ceremony of Award Honors: Early in December, 1998

6. How Programs will be Judged

Go to INDEX

  1. ENTRY CATEGORY: Programs will be judged as to whether they solve a given subject correctly with a checking program to be published.
  2. SPEED CATEGORY: Programs will first be judged on how fast and efficiently they solve the given subject. Next, the algorithm, the structure of the program and the documentation will be judged.
  3. IDEA CATEGORY: We will first screen programs using the documentation submitted, then check the programs. We will take the idea and documentation quality into account in the judgment. We may also ask the programmer to check the program operation themselves.
The environments for running your program are as follows:

  1. ENTRY CATEGORY: A UNIX workstation
  2. SPEED CATEGORY: A UNIX shared memory parallel processing server
  3. IDEA CATEGORY: Either environment (1) or (2), depending on the program
