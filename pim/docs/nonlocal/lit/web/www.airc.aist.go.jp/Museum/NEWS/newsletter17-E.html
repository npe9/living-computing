1. Introduction

The Research Institute for Advanced Information Technology (AITEC) of
the Japan Information Processing Development Center (JIPDEC) has been
disseminating the fifth-generation computer technology that was
developed in the ``Fifth Generation Computer Systems (FGCS) Project
(1982-1992)'', and the ``FGCS Follow-on Project (1993-1995)'' that
were mainly carried out by the Institute for New Generation Computer
Technology (ICOT).

ICOT released ICOT Free Software (IFS), then AITEC also released it in
fiscal 1996. This advanced software resource has been enriched by
AITEC's contract research project since fiscal 1996. The software
developed under AITEC's contract research project is called ``Extended
IFS''.

In fiscal 1998, AITEC is inviting proposals for Brushup of IFS and
extended IFS.

Brushup of IFS and extended IFS means:

	* Improvement of operability of software
	* Expansion of user's manual and/or examples
	* Improvement of document explaining the program and/or its
	  algorithms

The aim of Brushup is to improvement of utility of the software
and hence promote its use, not only in research but also for practical
applications, education and so forth.

Please note that the contract research project for fiscal 1998 is
quite different from those up until the last fiscal year. Contractors
may do research and improve the functionality of the software, but the
main purpose of the contract research project is to improve the
accessibility of the software.

Applicants need not be researchers who developed the software
originally, need not be Japanese, and need not be resident in Japan.

AITEC will make an archive for the improved software that will be made
during this fiscal year, namely, IFS and extended IFS. AITEC will open
the archived software to the public as soon as possible.

The Department of FGCS Technology that handles the contract research
project is planned to be disbanded at the end of this fiscal year.
Thus, the contractor will be expected to submit improved software and
related documents no later than the end of January 1999.

2.3 Number of research contracts to be awarded


In fiscal 1998, we will support approximately 15 projects.


2.4 Scope and type of support


(1) Approximately 1,500,000 Japanese Yen will be given to each of the
    supported projects. This award will be provided in Japanese Yen,
    and shall include all overheads for the university, if necessary.

(2) The contractor will be contacted in due course concerning the
    method of payment and handling of resulting software.

(3) Only one-year projects will be accepted.

(4) The contractor is requested to permit public release of the
    software by AITEC under the terms and conditions similar to ICOT
    Free Software (IFS); the copyright of the portion of the software
    that the contractor improves remains with the contractor, or a
    person designated by the contractor. Please refer to

     http://www.icot.or.jp/AITEC/IFS/list/using-IFS-E.html

    for the terms and conditions for IFS, or please contact us to
    obtain them.

2.5 Obligations of the Contractor


(1) An intermediate progress report of about 250 words in English must
    be sent to AITEC around the end of October 1998 (the precise
    deadline will be informed later) by e-mail. This report should
    include:

     * Purpose
     * Research method
     * Intermediate results
     * Future plan for this fiscal year

(2) The project final report of about 30 pages written in English must
    be sent to AITEC by the end of January 1999. The report should be
    sent by e-mail. The resulting software and files such as README,
    user manuals, and installation manuals should be sent to AITEC by
    ftp.

    The deadline for submitting the software must be strictly
    observed.

    Copyright of the report shall be transferred to AITEC and the
    Mechanical Social Systems Foundation, while the copyright of the
    software will remain with the contractor or a person designated by
    the principal investigator.

(3) A plan of expenses must be sent to AITEC. Contractors are expected
    to follow the expense guidelines that will be informed later.

(4) Accounts must be kept and copies of receipts retained, and must be
    sent to AITEC by the end of February 1999.

(5) In addition to the above, the state of progress of research and so
    forth must be reported to AITEC if requested.

2.6 Period of support


The research period for the awarded projects will start from June 16,
1988 and continue to January 31, 1999. Expenses for the research
activities for the project during this period are permitted. Thus, the
period of support is from June 16, 1998 to 31st January, 1999.

Message From the Editorial Desk

 This issue announces the AITEC Research Funding Program for fiscal 1998.

Before releasing the software developed by projects awarded by the AITEC research funding program, we will install, execute and check the software, and check documents to increase the accessibility of the software. This will take several months.

The Department of FGCS Technology that handles the research funding program is planned to be disbanded at the end of this fiscal year. Thus, it is extremely difficult to acquire the time required to undertake such additional work. This is why AITEC requests the software to be submitted by the end of January 1999. Thus, the deadline for the submission is quite strict.

We look forward to receiving your application.

We will continue reporting on various activities, research, and achievements relating to AITEC through AITEC NEWS. If you have any news or information you would like to include in this publication, please send it to us at: aitec-news@icot.or.jp.

All the staff at AITEC hope to see you again soon in the next AITEC NEWS.
