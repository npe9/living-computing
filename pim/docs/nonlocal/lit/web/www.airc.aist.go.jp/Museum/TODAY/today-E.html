ICOT Today

Since April 1993, ICOT carried out two-year project called ``FGCS Follow-on project''. The aim of the project was to fuse FGCS technology into existing computer technology, and disseminate it as research infrastructure for future computer technology.

In the project, ICOT published an electric mail-based publication informing R&D and other activities including events that ICOT had called ``ICOT Today''.

Remark that all issues of ICOT Today was written in Japanese.
