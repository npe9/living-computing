   コピー: the same transfer-operation menu, default copy.
   拡大・縮小: a left double click selects the type, either magnify or expand
   (「ウインドウ・システムで提供しているmagnify とexpand」: both provided by the window system); default expand.
   回転: rotation. After the rectangle is given, a line follows the mouse from its lower-left corner; a
   click sets the angle, measured anticlockwise.
   閉領域塗り潰し: fill the closed region containing the clicked point with the current pattern. A left
   double click allows clipping to a given rectangle.
   消しゴム: the mouse becomes an eraser; hold the LEFT button to erase; (cont.)
