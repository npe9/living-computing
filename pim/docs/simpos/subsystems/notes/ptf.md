The package attributes are identical in all 21 files: `using_env = simpos`, `lversion = lib50`, `release = off`, plus a `version` of 4 words (MEASURED, `bin/hdr.py`). The `version` words (word 1 = day, words 2-3 = milliseconds) and each class's trailer stamps are one format: (day number, milliseconds in the day). The day number counts from **1901-01-01** (INFERENCE).
