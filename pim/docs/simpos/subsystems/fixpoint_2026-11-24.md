# Fixed-point search for SIMPOS, its subsystems, its manuals and Common ESP (2026-11-24)

The third search of the day, after `web_search_2026-11-24.md`, `web_search2_2026-11-24.md` and
`sweep_2026-11-24.md`. Those three each ran once. This one ran in **rounds**. Each round followed
every lead the previous round produced, in the held material and on the web. It stopped at a
**fixed point**: a round in which every new lead had been followed and none gave a new source or a
new fact.

All accesses were on 2026-11-24. Nobody was contacted, nothing was bought, no form was submitted,
no terms were accepted and no bot challenge was bypassed. "Opened" means the text or page images
were read. "Located" means only a catalogue or search record was seen.

> **No SIMPOS or CESP code, binary, tape image or software archive was found** in any round, held
> or on the web. Nothing is listed for the owner to fetch. No new SIMPOS method body (HELD-SOURCE)
> turned up; the only held SIMPOS source is still what `finds.md` §1-3 and §5a list.

## 1. Method

**The lead ledger.** It was seeded from everything learned so far: `../../psi_acquisition.md`
§1-§13, `../../archive_search_notes.md`, `../source_notes.md`, this directory's three earlier
records and its specs, `../trtm_table.tsv`, `../printed/coverage.tsv` and
`../../nonlocal/lit/SOURCES.tsv`. Every person, title, organisation, version, file name, path
stamp, place and "not searched" item became a lead with a status.

**Held material, each round.** Every archive under `pim/docs/nonlocal/` was unpacked again into
scratch: 312 archives, 1.0 GB. It was searched three ways:
- with regexes on raw bytes;
- with a Japanese-decoding grep (ISO-2022-JP, EUC-JP, Shift-JIS, UTF-8) for the ledger's names
  and titles;
- with a new usage census of the ESP code no earlier pass had mined (`tools/fe_census.py`).

**Web, round 1.** Three delegated searches ran in parallel: people; manual and book titles;
organisations, places, code and version strings. Their reports were checked against the files
here, and the sources they opened were re-fetched and read for the facts used below.

**Web, rounds 2-4.** These were run directly, with these tools:
- CiNii Research's OpenSearch JSON (its record pages answer HTTP 202, a JavaScript gate);
- the IPSJ repository's record API and file links;
- NDL Search OpenSearch and the NDL Digital item API;
- KAKEN;
- general web search.

**Reading.** Image-only IPSJ PDFs were rendered at 300 dpi and OCR'd (tesseract jpn+eng). Every
fact taken into a spec was checked on the page image or the held source line.

How far the fixed point holds:
- It covers the reachable places. The unreachable ones in §3 were tried in every round they
  applied to.
- Rounds 2-4 ran fewer queries per lead than round 1's delegated searches. A lead counts as
  followed when its name or title was queried in CiNii Research (and IPSJ or NDL where relevant)
  and the held material was grepped for it.

## 4. Who and what to ask (institutional routes only, ranked)

Nobody was contacted. Each route below is an institution's published enquiry channel; no
personal address was inferred.

1. **JAIST Library** (C42.4): the five 1985 PSI-I manuals, the ICOT Journal, and the PSI in its
   symbolic-computer collection, with "related manuals and materials". Ask for scans of
   MP3001-1/MP3002-1 and for a list of the preserved materials, including any later
   SIMPOS/PSI-II manuals or media.
2. **NDL** (reading room, or the transmission service by the owner):
   - the 通産省公報 registrations (SIMPOS 2.0, V4.0, 6.0, 5G-03-001);
   - bit 1990, parts 1-12, and bit 21(1);
   - 機械振興 1990/1991;
   - AI白書 1989-94;
   - ETL report 954 and 電総研彙報 54(8);
   - 沖電気研究開発 1985-91.
   These hold the release chronology and secondary descriptions, not the manuals.
3. **Mitsubishi Electric's IT group** (successor of 三菱電機東部コンピュータシステム): MTC sold
   CESP (1994, 「CESP言語2.0版」), wrote CIL, and employed the SIMPOS Librarian customisation
   authors. It is the most likely holder of SIMPOS-era sources outside ICOT. Its successor
   company is still not identified.
4. **AIST** (ICOT archive and ETL successor): ETL ran CESP (田島守彦's RLS port); 実近憲昭's group.
5. **Oki Electric**: AIR's CESP leads (中澤修, 山本明, 岩永理美子) and Quixote's SIMPOS
   interface (羽生田) came from Oki; an Oki PSI-II ran SIMPOS 6.2.
6. **Universities that received CESP** (`web_search2_2026-11-24.md` rank 4), and 津田塾大学 or
   UEC for the 1986-87 Tokyo study of the SIMPOS 2.52 source (青柳龍也, 田中英彦 lab).
7. **ASTEM** (CESP-Fortran, TR-P-027-91).
8. **Booksellers** (record only; buying needs the owner): bit 1990-01, 1990-07 and 1990-10, and
   『新しいOS』 (1989), on 日本の古本屋.
