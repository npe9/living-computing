# ALOHA packet-broadcast system

The University of Hawaii ALOHA system used radio packet broadcasting and
random access, with retransmission after collisions. Its packet-control units
evolved from hardwired terminal controllers to programmable Intel 8008 and
8080 designs. The book discusses pure and slotted ALOHA and its influence on
later local networks.

Bluespec implementation: `bsv/` (not started).

See [`plan.md`](plan.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md). The initial
source is Chapter 25 of *Computer Structures: Principles and Examples* (1982).
