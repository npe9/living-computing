# Intel 8086

The Intel 8086 was a 16-bit successor in Intel's microprocessor family. Its
variable-length instruction organization, segmented addressing, and expanded
register and data paths show how a microprocessor family increased capacity
while retaining evolutionary compatibility.

Bluespec implementation: bsv/ (not started).

See [`plan.md`](plan.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md). The initial
source is Chapter 37, printed p. 615, of *Computer Structures: Principles and
Examples* (1982).
