# Hewlett-Packard 9820

The HP 9820 was a programmable calculator in Hewlett-Packard's 9810/20/30
series. Its vertically encoded microprogrammed control and calculator-oriented
instruction organization illustrate the family's shift from horizontal
calculator microcode toward a reusable minicomputer-style architecture.

Bluespec implementation: bsv/ (not started).

See [`plan.md`](plan.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md). The initial
source is Chapter 49, printed p. 801, of *Computer Structures: Principles and
Examples* (1982).
