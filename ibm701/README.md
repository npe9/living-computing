# IBM 701 (1952)

IBM's first commercial scientific computer ("Defense Calculator"). 36-bit words, 2048 words Williams-tube store, half-word (18-bit) instructions, fixed-point sign-magnitude arithmetic, magnetic drum and tape. Direct ancestor of the 704/709/7090.

Bluespec implementation: `bsv/` (not started).

Sources: *IBM 701 Principles of Operation* (Form 24-6042).
