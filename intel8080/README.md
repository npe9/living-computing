# Intel 8080

The Intel 8080 was an 8-bit microprocessor with a richer register and I/O
organization than the 8008. Its low cost and general-purpose instruction set
made it a widely used microcomputer processor and a major step in the
microprocessor family traced by the book.

Bluespec implementation: bsv/ (not started).

See [`plan.md`](plan.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md). The initial
source is Chapter 37, printed p. 615, of *Computer Structures: Principles and
Examples* (1982).
