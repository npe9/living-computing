# IBM 3030 series

The IBM 3030 series belongs to the planned compatible-system families
discussed with System/360, System/370, and the 4300 series. Its significance
in the book is the way a common architectural interface spans implementations
with different cost, storage, and performance characteristics.

Bluespec implementation: bsv/ (not started).

See [`plan.md`](plan.md) and [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md). The initial
source is Chapter 52 of *Computer Structures: Principles and Examples* (1982).
